What the app does

Aloe Cleaner scans your iPhone photo library and groups what can usually be removed into four categories: exact duplicates, similar photos from burst sequences, screenshots, and videos over 100 MB. You choose what to delete, and the app frees up the storage those items occupied.

Scanning is free. Deleting requires an active Aloe Pro subscription.

Deletion is your decision

You decide what gets deleted. Review the selection before you confirm — iOS shows its own confirmation dialog, and nothing is removed until you accept it.

Deleted items move to Recently Deleted in Photos and are removed permanently after 30 days. Within those 30 days you can restore them yourself from the Photos app. Afterwards recovery is impossible, and we are not able to restore anything for you.

Aloe groups items by visual similarity. Grouping is a suggestion, not a judgement about which photo matters to you — always look before you confirm.
